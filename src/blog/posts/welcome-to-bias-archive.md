Every semester, marketing students are asked to learn frameworks — the 4Ps, AIDA, the customer
journey — largely in the abstract. This blog is an attempt to do the opposite: take one
real, current example each week and hold it up against the theory to see what actually explains
what's happening.

## What to expect

Expect short, focused entries — not exhaustive case studies. Each post will pick a single concept
(a positioning strategy, a pricing model, a platform algorithm change) and a single brand or
campaign currently doing something interesting with it.

## Why it matters

Marketing theory ages fast in some places and barely at all in others. Tracking both, in public,
over a semester, is a useful way to find out which is which.
